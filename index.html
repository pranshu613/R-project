Overview

What do these columns mean?
1 day / 1 year
How much the price changed since the previous close, and over the last 252 trading days.
Volatility
How much the price typically swings over a year. Higher means a bumpier ride.
Worst drop
The biggest fall from a previous high point in the period.
Sharpe
Return earned for each unit of risk, after subtracting a risk-free rate. Higher is better.
Rank
Position by 1-year return, where 1 is the best.

How the stocks move together

Correlation of daily returns. 1 means they move in step, 0 means unrelated, and below 0 means they tend to move in opposite directions.
